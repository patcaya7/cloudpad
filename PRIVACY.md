# CloudPad Privacy Policy

CloudPad stores notes and preferences locally on the user's device by default.

Cloud synchronization is optional. When a user chooses to sign in and enable
sync, CloudPad uses Supabase authentication and stores the user's account
identifier and synchronized note data to provide that service.

CloudPad does not display advertising and does not sell personal information.
Data is used only to provide local note storage, optional synchronization, and
the related support and security functions of the application.

For privacy questions or requests, please open an issue at
https://github.com/patcaya7/cloudpad/issues.
